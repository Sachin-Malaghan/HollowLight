A small light in a grey wood

The rain has not stopped for a long time. The forest is the colour of ash, and somewhere past the last of the trees a lamp post stands unlit.

A child in a scarf carries the only warm thing left — a lantern — through ten stretches of wood, warehouse and railway. A stray dog decides to come too. Jaws hide in the grass. Logs swing on old ropes. Rotten branches give way a breath after you land. Black water swallows anything that sinks, and floats anything that doesn't.

There are no words to read and nothing to buy. Just the rain, the dark, and the little circle of amber you carry with you.

Ten handcrafted levels

From the edge of the wood, through a warehouse and a railway station, to the village at dawn. Levers, pressure plates, ladders, drawbridges, tools to carry — puzzles you have to think about.

A dog that helps

It barks at the traps it smells, holds a plate down while you run for the door, squeezes through gaps you cannot, and points when you are stuck.

Drawn and sounded as you play

Every tree, raindrop and ember is generated live, and so is every sound — the rain, the footsteps, the quiet score. No images, no recordings.

Made for your hands

Big, forgiving touch controls on phones and tablets. Keyboard and gamepad on PC. Checkpoints are close together; death is a breath, not a punishment.

Nothing taken from you

No ads, no accounts, no tracking, no in-app purchases. Your progress stays on your device.

From the game

On PC

Arrows or A D to move, Space W or ↑ to jump — hold it for a full jump, tap it for a short hop. Esc pauses. Any Xbox or PlayStation controller works too.
